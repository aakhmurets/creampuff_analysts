Model Methodology

Base Revenue Calculation

  • FY2025 total revenue: $19.49B (reported)
  • US-only estimate: ~$15.73B (81% of total)
  • Excludes International segment (~$2.84B)
  • Canada portion (~6% of NA Retail) excluded
  • Daily baseline: $43.1M (365 days)

Channel Mix Assumptions

  • Traditional Retail: 82% ($12.89B)
  • E-commerce: 9% ($1.42B) - per GMI disclosures
  • Foodservice/Other: 9% ($1.42B)
  • E-commerce grows +15% in Q4 (holiday)
  • Foodservice peaks in summer (+10%)

Seasonality Factors

  • Jan-Feb: +8% (New Year resolutions, cereal peak)
  • Mar-Apr: +3% (Easter baking)
  • May-Jun: -8% (summer slump begins)
  • Jul-Aug: -5% (vacation effect, back-to-school late Aug)
  • Sep: +2% (school routines resume)
  • Oct-Dec: +12-18% (holiday baking, entertaining)

Day-of-Week Patterns

  • Saturday: 17% of weekly (peak shopping)
  • Sunday: 15% of weekly
  • Friday: 14.5% of weekly
  • Mon-Thu: 13-14% each
  • Paycheck effect: +5% on 1st & 15th

Event Multipliers

  • Super Bowl week: +35%
  • Valentine's week: +18%
  • Easter week: +28%
  • Memorial/July 4th/Labor Day: +12-15%
  • Back-to-school (Aug 1-20): +20%
  • Halloween week: +22%
  • Thanksgiving week: +58%
  • Christmas week: +45%

Category Weighting

  • Cereal: Peaks Jan-Feb, dips Jun
  • Snacks: Super Bowl, summer BBQ peaks
  • Baking: Oct-Dec holiday peak (+40%)
  • Yogurt: Steady year-round
  • Pet Food: Consistent, slight Q4 bump

Critical Assumptions & Limitations

  • This is a MODEL, not actual data. General Mills does not publicly disclose daily or weekly revenue figures. All daily values are estimated based on annual/quarterly actuals distributed using behavioral patterns.
  • US-only approximation: North America segment includes Canada (~6-7%). We estimate US-only by excluding this portion, but precise split is not disclosed.
  • Channel mix is estimated: E-commerce at 9% is based on GMI's disclosed "approximately 9% of sales" as of 2020, with modest growth assumed. Actual 2025 mix may differ.
  • Seasonality patterns based on industry data: We applied CPG industry seasonal patterns (Circana, NRF data) and cereal-specific trends. Actual GMI seasonality may vary by brand/category.
  • Event impacts are estimates: Multipliers for holidays/events based on retail industry benchmarks, not GMI-specific data. Actual lift varies by product category.
  • Day-of-week patterns from general grocery data: Based on Gallup consumer spending and grocery industry patterns, not GMI-specific POS data.
  • No promotional/pricing effects: Model assumes steady pricing. Actual sales spike during promotions (BOGO, coupons) are not captured.
  • Foodservice timing simplified: Institutional (schools, hospitals) has different patterns vs. restaurants - model uses blended approach.
  • Calendar 2025 ≠ Fiscal 2025: GMI's fiscal year ends in May. This model uses calendar year 2025 for simplicity.
