! Data Accuracy Concerns & Limitations

  • 1. US vs. Global Segmentation: General Mills reports "North America" figures which include Canada. Pure US-only data is not publicly disclosed separately. North America Retail includes Canada (estimated ~5-7% of segment). The Canada yogurt divestiture in Q3 FY2025 further complicates year-over-year comparisons.
  • 2. Fiscal Year Timing: General Mills' fiscal year ends in late May, not December. FY2025 ended May 25, 2025, and FY2024 ended May 28, 2024. Calendar year comparisons may be misleading.
  • 3. Segment Reclassifications: Acquisitions (Edgard & Cooper, Whitebridge Pet Brands) and divestitures (Helper meals, Canada yogurt) affect comparability. Organic growth rates exclude these impacts but may not reflect true business performance.
  • 4. Category-Level Data: Detailed US category breakdowns (Cereal, Snacks, Meals & Baking, Yogurt) are not fully disclosed. Estimates based on operating unit performance descriptions ("down low-single digits", "up mid-single digits").
  • 5. Market Share Data Sources: Cereal market share figures come from third-party sources (Circana, Euromonitor) which may use different methodologies. The 30-31% market share is for total US cereal; brand-level shares may vary.
  • 6. Stock Price Volatility: Stock prices shown are representative snapshots. Intraday and daily fluctuations are not captured. All-time high of $81.01 (May 2023) vs. current ~$44-46 represents significant value decline.
  • 7. Estimates Indicated: Values marked "(est.)" are calculated estimates based on reported percentages and segment ratios, not official disclosures.
  • 8. News & Analyst Subjectivity: Analyst price targets ($53-81) and growth forecasts vary significantly. News coverage may emphasize certain aspects over others.
